# Engineering Automation and Abundance Acceptance

1. Establish a baseline method, quality, lifecycle-cost assumptions and evidence gaps.
2. Generate alternatives subject to explicit hard engineering constraints.
3. Reject a cheaper option that fails a hard requirement.
4. Present feasible trade-offs, evidence, sources and uncertainty; claim “least cost” only when supported.
5. Preserve baseline and alternatives separately until approval.
6. Preserve provenance across reconstruction, assessment, environment/mini-app execution and verification.
7. Route an unresolved gap through a permissioned demonstration or Arena; validate any candidate capability on held-out cases.
8. Measure human intervention reduction only alongside verified quality/safety.
9. Compare actual vs predicted cost/time, including human and operations where data permits.
10. Block or escalate when evidence is insufficient or qualified judgment remains required.

Intervention reduction alone is never sufficient for acceptance.
