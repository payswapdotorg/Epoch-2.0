# W035 — Engineering automation and abundance evaluation

## Dependency
W020 + W022 + W024 + W031 + W032 + W033 + W034.

## Owned surfaces
- packages/epoch-engineering-objective/**
- qa/epoch-engineering-abundance/**

## Goal
Measure whether Epoch is automating more engineering work and delivering required outcomes faster or at lower lifecycle cost without degrading safety, quality or accountability.

## Deliverables
- named task-family denominator;
- completion and verified output-quality metrics;
- baseline cost/time and human-intervention measures;
- escalation and capability-reuse rates;
- hard-constraint/safety regression measures;
- predicted-versus-actual cost/quality outcomes;
- end-to-end case spanning reconstruction, assessment, environment/mini-app, verification and gap closure;
- limitations and next bottleneck.

## Acceptance
- One full case preserves provenance across lifecycle stages.
- Lower cost is claimed only for feasible solutions.
- Automation definitions/denominators are explicit.
- Fewer interventions count as progress only when verified quality/safety remains acceptable.
- Remaining human/Arena tasks are visible and not counted as automated successes.
